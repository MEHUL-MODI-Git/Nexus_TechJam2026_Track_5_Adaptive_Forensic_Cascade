## MOMENT 1 — The problem · TAB 2, slide 2

**DO:** Show slide 2. Do not click anything.

**SAY:**
> "Anyone can generate a convincing fake photo in seconds, so platforms need to catch them

## MOMENT 4 — How it works · TAB 2, slide 3

**DO:** Switch to slides, show slide 3.

**SAY:**
> "Here's why that's fixable. A detector alone can't tell 'this looks real' from 'this got damaged on
> the way here'.
> So before judging the picture we measure what was done to it — how blurry, how compressed, how
> noisy. A frozen expert detector gives its opinion. Then our part, a correction layer of one
> thousand eight hundred and twenty-seven numbers, combines the two and corrects the verdict.
> The demo, the command line and our testing all run the same code."

**EXPECT:** five numbered rows, top to bottom: Canonical decode, Frozen expert — Community Forensics
384, Damage descriptors, **Reliability router — the contribution**, Verdict + confidence grade. The
bottom two rows are highlighted brighter than the top three.

## MOMENT 5 — It checks itself · TAB 1

**DO:**
1. Back to the demo. The compressed image is still loaded — do not re-upload.
2. Scroll down to **Stress-test this image** and click it.
3. Wait about 3 seconds. Then click **Stress results table** to expand it.

**SAY:**
> "This is the part that matters most. A detector that's confidently wrong is dangerous, because you
> can't tell a good answer from a bad one.
> So the system checks itself. It makes twenty damaged versions of this picture, re-runs its verdict
> on each, and counts how many still agree.
> Eighteen of twenty. So it grades itself medium confidence, and tells you what that's worth: answers
> this solid were right ninety-five percent of the time. And it names the damage that would break
> it."

**EXPECT:** `Verdict retention 18 / 20 stress conditions`. `Forensic reliability: MEDIUM — verdicts at
this retention were correct for 94.9% of held-out sources`. A bar chart. In the table, exactly two
rows marked **FLIPPED**: `blur_s2.0` and `resize_0.25`.

**DO:** Show slide 4, speak, press **→**, show slide 5, speak.
