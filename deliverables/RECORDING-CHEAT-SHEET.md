## MOMENT 3 — Where it breaks · TAB 1

**DO:**
1. Scroll back up, drag `deliverables/video-assets/bird_jpeg_q30.png` onto the upload box
   (it replaces the first image).
2. Click **Analyze image**. Wait for the panel to fill.
3. **Scroll so the whole verdict card is framed** — from the `AI-GENERATED` line down to the yellow
   DEFERRED box. All of it fits at 125%.
4. Now start speaking.

**SAY:**
> "Now the same picture, saved as an ordinary compressed JPEG — what happens when you send a photo
> through a messaging app.
> There are two scores on this card, and the difference between them is the whole project.
> This one is the old detector on its own: zero point zero one nine, on a scale where anything under
> a half means 'real'. On its own it has been fooled.
> This one is ours. We take that same score, notice the picture was compressed, and correct it to
> nought point nine four — the verdict at the top. Still AI-generated. Still right.
> And it goes further: it says its own confidence is below its bar, so it asks for a human rather
> than guessing."

**EXPECT:** verdict still **AI-GENERATED**, `p_fake` **0.9458**. The line
`Primary CF-384 alone: 0.0191 → after router correction: 0.9458 (+0.9267)`. Then
`Self-assessed reliability: 0.788`, then
`Detected image history: JPEG compression (99% confidence)` with a warning triangle, then a
**yellow DEFERRED box** reading "low self-assessed reliability".

**Why the card says AI-GENERATED while you say the detector was fooled — read once, don't say it:**
`0.0191` is the OLD detector scoring alone. Under 0.5, so by itself it would call this real: fooled.
`0.9458` is YOUR system after the correction, and that is what the big `AI-GENERATED` heading
reflects. The big verdict being *right* is the point; the sub-score being *wrong* is what you are
pointing at. Two numbers, two different things.
